# Core Concepts

## Roles

Roles are enduring identities or areas of responsibility.

Examples:

- Consultant with Dr. Nagendra
- Founding team member of NaFDEc
- Builder of Emperor's PoV
- Parent
- Writer
- Researcher

Roles are not tasks. Roles are fulfilled over time.

## Goals

Goals are desired outcomes.

Goals may belong to one role or span multiple roles.

Examples:

- Create passive income
- Build a director-level role in a startup
- Become a full-time writer
- Maintain financial stability
- Create fulfilling family projects

## Initiatives

Initiatives are long-term or very long-term containers of meaningful work.

Examples:

- Support Dr. Nagendra's work
- Build NaFDEc
- Build Emperor's PoV
- Develop writing practice

An initiative can belong to multiple roles and support multiple goals.

## Deliverables

Deliverables are tangible outcomes inside initiatives.

Examples:

- Proposal document
- Marketing collateral
- Invoice system
- Knowledge graph prototype
- Weekly review process

## Efforts

Efforts are medium-term bodies of work required to produce deliverables.

Examples:

- Research
- Drafting
- Design
- Technical implementation
- Review and editing

## Actions

Actions are short-term executable steps.

Examples:

- Write first draft
- Create Canva layout
- Send invoice
- Prepare meeting notes

## Sub-actions

Sub-actions are very small steps.

Examples:

- Open Canva
- Export PDF
- Email client
- Create folder

## Temporal Nature

All items have a temporal aspect.

If work does not move forward, it may age, lose importance, or fade.

Fading is not failure. Fading is a respectful way to release interests that are no longer alive.
